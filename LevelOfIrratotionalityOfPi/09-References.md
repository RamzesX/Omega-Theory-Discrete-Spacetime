# Module 09 — References

## Core Results (Used in Proofs)

[Euler 1737] L. Euler, "De fractionibus continuis dissertatio," *Commentarii academiae scientiarum Petropolitanae* **9** (1737), 98–137. Establishes the CF expansion of $e$.

[Lagrange 1770] J.-L. Lagrange, "Additions au mémoire sur la résolution des équations numériques," *Miscellanea Taurinensia* **4** (1770). Eventually periodic CF $\Leftrightarrow$ quadratic irrational.

[Lindemann 1882] F. Lindemann, "Über die Zahl $\pi$," *Mathematische Annalen* **20** (1882), 213–225. Transcendence of $\pi$.

[Kuzmin 1928] R. O. Kuzmin, "On a problem of Gauss," *Doklady Akad. Nauk SSSR*, Ser. A (1928), 375–380. First proof of the Gauss–Kuzmin theorem.

[Lévy 1929] P. Lévy, "Sur les lois de probabilité dont dépendent les quotients complets et incomplets d'une fraction continue," *Bull. Soc. Math. France* **57** (1929), 178–194.

[Morse & Hedlund 1940] M. Morse and G. A. Hedlund, "Symbolic dynamics II: Sturmian trajectories," *Amer. J. Math.* **62** (1940), 1–42. Block complexity bound $p(n) \geq n+1$ for non-periodic sequences.

[Rokhlin 1961] V. A. Rokhlin, "Exact endomorphisms of a Lebesgue space," *Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR, Ser. Mat.* **25** (1961), 499–530. Entropy formula yielding $h(T,\gamma) = \pi^2/(6\ln 2)$.

[Lochs 1964] G. Lochs, "Vergleich der Genauigkeit von Dezimalbruch und Kettenbruch," *Abh. Math. Sem. Univ. Hamburg* **27** (1964), 142–144.

[Wirsing 1974] E. Wirsing, "On the theorem of Gauss–Kusmin–Lévy and a Frobenius-type theorem for function spaces," *Acta Arithmetica* **24** (1974), 507–528. Spectral gap $|\lambda_2| \approx 0.3037$.

[Babenko 1978] K. I. Babenko, "On a problem of Gauss," *Soviet Math. Dokl.* **19** (1978), 136–140.

## Supporting Results

[Roth 1955] K. F. Roth, "Rational approximations to algebraic numbers," *Mathematika* **2** (1955), 1–20. Irrationality measure $\mu = 2$ for all algebraic irrationals. (Fields Medal, 1958.)

[Adamczewski & Bugeaud 2005] B. Adamczewski and Y. Bugeaud, "On the complexity of algebraic numbers, II: Continued fractions," *Acta Math.* **195** (2005), 1–20.

[Bugeaud 2013] Y. Bugeaud, "Automatic continued fractions are transcendental or quadratic," *Ann. Sci. École Norm. Sup.* **46** (2013), 1005–1022.

[Adamczewski, Bugeaud & Davison 2006] B. Adamczewski, Y. Bugeaud, and L. Davison, "Continued fractions and transcendental numbers," *Ann. Inst. Fourier* **56** (2006), 2093–2113.

[Salikhov 2008] V. Kh. Salikhov, "On the irrationality measure of $\pi$," *Russian Math. Surveys* **63** (2008), 570–572.

[Zeilberger & Zudilin 2020] D. Zeilberger and W. Zudilin, "The irrationality measure of $\pi$ is at most 7.103205334137...," *Moscow J. Combin. Number Th.* **9** (2020), 407–419.

[Bailey & Crandall 2001] D. H. Bailey and R. E. Crandall, "On the random character of fundamental constant expansions," *Experimental Math.* **10** (2001), 175–190.

[Bailey, Borwein & Plouffe 1997] D. Bailey, P. Borwein, and S. Plouffe, "On the rapid computation of various polylogarithmic constants," *Math. Comp.* **66** (1997), 903–913.

[Borwein & Borwein 1987] J. M. Borwein and P. B. Borwein, *Pi and the AGM: A Study in Analytic Number Theory and Computational Complexity*, Wiley, 1987.
