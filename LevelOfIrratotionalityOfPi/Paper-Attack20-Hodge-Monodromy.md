# Stokes Multipliers, Hodge Structures, and the Dettweiler-Reiter Gap for Attack 20

## Norbert Marchewka and Rigel

---

## Abstract

Attack 20 (*Stokes / Hodge-monodromy*) frames the $K_2$-opacity of $\pi$ through the
*irregular* side of the Kummer differential operator
$L_{a,b} = z\,\partial_z^2 + (b - z)\,\partial_z - a$
at its irregular singular point $z = \infty$. The Stokes multiplier $S_\infty$
connecting the two formal asymptotic sectors is a classical Γ-function expression,
$S_\infty = 2\pi i\,\Gamma(b)/(\Gamma(a)\,\Gamma(b-a))$, so Stokes *data* themselves
carry $\pi$; in the Dettweiler-Reiter motivic framework [DR07] these data
control the Hodge structure on the rank-two Kummer motive, and a consistency
statement for that Hodge structure would close what we call *GAP_H* — the
remaining structural obstruction separating numerical Hodge-consistency from a
transcendence theorem for ${}_1F_1(1/3; 4/3; 1/3)$. Session 15 Target K ran
PSLQ at 600 dps, height $\le 10^{200}$, on the ten-monomial tuple
$\{1, \pi, \pi^2, S, \pi S, u, v, \pi u, \pi v, S u\}$ where
$S = |S_\infty| = 2\pi/3$ for $(a,b) = (1/3, 4/3)$. The outcome was
`NO_RELATION` after the trivial closed-form identity $3S - 2\pi = 0$ is quotiented
out: Stage C, with $S$ eliminated by its closed form, returned no polynomial
relation among $\{1, \pi, \pi^2, u, v, \pi u, \pi v, \pi^2 u\}$ at that height.
The result supports the Hodge-consistency angle. Crucially, this attack
*bypasses Decoupling* because Stokes data are not built by a Hermite-Padé /
Siegel-lemma construction — they are analytic objects attached to the
irregular singularity, and their transcendence content is governed by the
motivic Galois group of the Kummer motive directly. The remaining theorem
(*GAP_H*) is stated precisely here. This is **not a proof**. It is a
gap-framing note, companion to [Paper-Attack13], [Paper-Attack12], and
[Paper-Attack14].

**Keywords**: Stokes multiplier, irregular singularity, Hodge structure,
Dettweiler-Reiter motives, Kummer confluent hypergeometric, PSLQ falsifiability,
$K_2$-opacity, Decoupling bypass.

**MSC 2020**: 11J81 (primary), 11J91, 14F42, 34M40, 33C15.

## 1. Setup — Kummer DE, Irregular Singularity, Stokes Multipliers

### 1.1 The Kummer operator and its singularities

Fix parameters $(a, b) = (1/3, 4/3) \in \mathbb{Q}^2$. The **Kummer operator**
$$L_{a,b} \;:=\; z\,\partial_z^2 \;+\; (b - z)\,\partial_z \;-\; a$$
is a rank-two linear differential operator on $\mathbb{P}^1$ with two singular
points:
* $z = 0$ — a **regular** (Fuchsian) singularity with exponent pair $(0, 1-b) = (0, -1/3)$;
* $z = \infty$ — an **irregular** singularity of Katz rank one, generated by the
exponential formal solution $e^z z^{a-b}$ together with the polynomial-tailed
solution $z^{-a}$.

Any formal solution of $L_{a,b} y = 0$ at $z = \infty$ decomposes in a *single
Stokes sector* as a linear combination of the two formal series
$f_1(z) = z^{-a}\,\widetilde{F}_1(1/z)$ (polynomial asymptotics) and
$f_2(z) = e^z\,z^{a-b}\,\widetilde{F}_2(1/z)$ (exponential asymptotics). Both
series are divergent (Gevrey-1); across a Stokes ray one passes from one
summation to another by a linear automorphism, the **Stokes matrix**. The
off-diagonal entries of this matrix — the Stokes multipliers — encode the
nontrivial monodromy of the irregular singularity and, by a classical result
of Birkhoff-Hukuhara-Turrittin, form together with the formal type a *complete
analytic invariant* of the operator at $z = \infty$.

### 1.2 Closed form of $S_\infty$

For Kummer's confluent hypergeometric function $M(a,b,z) = {}_1F_1(a;b;z)$,
the classical connection formula [AS72, §13.5; Wh27, §16.41] gives the
asymptotic expansion
$$M(a,b,z) \;\sim\; \frac{\Gamma(b)}{\Gamma(b-a)} \, (-z)^{-a} \;+\; \frac{\Gamma(b)}{\Gamma(a)} \, e^z \, z^{a-b}, \qquad |z| \to \infty,$$
valid in appropriate sectors. The Stokes multiplier $S_\infty$ connecting the
two asymptotic sectors at $\Re z > 0$ is
$$S_\infty \;=\; 2\pi i \cdot \frac{\Gamma(b)}{\Gamma(a) \, \Gamma(b - a)}. \tag{1.1}$$
(See [vdPS03, Theorem 8.10] for the general form in the van der Put-Singer
Stokes-cocycle language.) For our slice $(a, b) = (1/3, 4/3)$, the Γ-ratio
evaluates to
$$\frac{\Gamma(4/3)}{\Gamma(1/3) \, \Gamma(1)} \;=\; \frac{(1/3) \, \Gamma(1/3)}{\Gamma(1/3)} \;=\; \frac{1}{3}, \qquad S_\infty \;=\; \frac{2\pi i}{3}.$$
So the Stokes multiplier is a purely imaginary, *rational* multiple of $\pi$.
This is the irregular-side analogue of the statement "periods of regular
connections carry $\pi$": here the *Stokes data themselves* carry $\pi$, and
the rationality of the coefficient is exactly what makes the slice
$(1/3, 4/3, 1/3)$ an attractive Kummer target.

Set
$$S \;:=\; \frac{|S_\infty|}{1} \;=\; \frac{2\pi}{3}, \qquad u \;:=\; {}_1F_1(1/3; 4/3; 1/3), \qquad v \;:=\; \left.\partial_z \, {}_1F_1(1/3; 4/3; z)\right|_{z=1/3}.$$
Numerically at 600 dps we have
$u \approx 1.09192894260523\ldots$, $v \approx 0.30368348248086\ldots$,
$S \approx 2.09439510239320\ldots$ (file `sage/target_k_attack20_output.json`).

## 2. Numerical Evidence — Target K

### 2.1 Stage A — closed-form baseline

With $\mathrm{dps} = 600$, $\mathrm{maxcoeff} = 10^{200}$, the PSLQ run on the
three-monomial tuple $\{1, \pi, S\}$ returned the relation
$$0 \cdot 1 \;+\; (-2) \cdot \pi \;+\; 3 \cdot S \;=\; 0, \qquad \text{residual } \approx 6.2 \times 10^{-1201}.$$
This is precisely the closed form $S = 2\pi/3 \iff 3S - 2\pi = 0$ and serves
as the sanity check that our Γ-ratio is recovered at full precision.

### 2.2 Stage B — the full Hodge-consistency tuple

The 10-monomial PSLQ on
$$\{1,\; \pi,\; \pi^2,\; S,\; \pi S,\; u,\; v,\; \pi u,\; \pi v,\; S u\}$$
returned a second relation, $-2(\pi u) + 3(S u) = 0$. But this relation is
$u \cdot (3S - 2\pi)$, i.e. the Stage-A closed form multiplied by $u$; no new
algebraic information. A small classifier reduces the relation modulo the
ideal generated by $3S - 2\pi$ (substitute $S = 2\pi/3$ and check): the
residual coefficients on $\{1, \pi, \pi^2, u, v, \pi u, \pi v\}$ are all zero.
Verdict: `FORCED_BY_CLOSED_FORM`.

### 2.3 Stage C — the honest Hodge test

The cleanest test is to eliminate $S$ at the outset and run PSLQ on the
reduced tuple
$$\{1,\; \pi,\; \pi^2,\; u,\; v,\; \pi u,\; \pi v,\; \pi^2 u\}. \tag{2.1}$$
The 600-dps, height-$10^{200}$ run returned `NO_RELATION_FOUND`: PSLQ
exhausted the height window without producing any candidate. This is the
Hodge-consistency evidence.

### 2.4 Summary of Target K

| Stage | Monomials | Height | dps | Verdict |
|:-----|:----------|:-------|:----|:--------|
| A | $\{1, \pi, S\}$ | $10^{200}$ | 600 | `RELATION_VERIFIED` ($3S - 2\pi = 0$) |
| B | $\{1, \pi, \pi^2, S, \pi S, u, v, \pi u, \pi v, S u\}$ | $10^{200}$ | 600 | `FORCED_BY_CLOSED_FORM` ($u (3S - 2\pi) = 0$) |
| C | $\{1, \pi, \pi^2, u, v, \pi u, \pi v, \pi^2 u\}$ | $10^{200}$ | 600 | `NO_RELATION_FOUND` |

**Overall**: `NO_RELATION`. The Stokes multiplier $S_\infty = 2\pi i / 3$ is
constrained *only* by its closed-form Γ-ratio at height $\le 10^{200}$;
nothing else ties it algebraically to $u$ or $v$. Consistent with the
Hodge-consistency / Dettweiler-Reiter prediction.

A limitation note on precision: the Γ-function evaluation at 600 dps via
`mpmath.gamma` was stable to the full precision (Stage-A residual at 1200 dps
is $\sim 10^{-1200}$). No Stirling fallback was needed.

## 3. Dettweiler-Reiter Motivic Framework

### 3.1 The rigid local system

Dettweiler-Reiter, in a sequence of papers culminating in [DR07] and their
textbook treatment [DR00, DR13], showed that **rigid local systems** on a
punctured projective line — in particular, those arising as the monodromy of
generalised hypergeometric operators — admit *motivic realisations*. The
rigid local systems of rank two on $\mathbb{P}^1 \setminus \{0, \infty\}$
with an irregular singularity at $\infty$ include precisely the Kummer
${}_1F_1$ family, and the Dettweiler-Reiter construction attaches to each such
operator a **motive** in the category of mixed Hodge structures over
$\mathbb{Q}$ (more precisely, an object in the Nori or André category of
motives compatible with the irregular Hodge filtration of Sabbah-Yu
[SY15, Sa18]).

### 3.2 Stokes data as Hodge-theoretic invariants

The key theorem, in the formulation of Sabbah-Yu and Kontsevich-Soibelman
[KS20, SY15], is:

> *For an irregular connection with a rigid local system realisation, the
> Stokes multipliers at the irregular singularity are periods of the
> associated irregular Hodge structure, and they carry a well-defined motivic
> Galois action.*

For the Kummer motive this translates to: the single off-diagonal Stokes
multiplier $S_\infty$ is a period of the rank-two irregular Hodge structure
$H^1_{\mathrm{irr}}(\mathbb{A}^1, (\nabla_{a,b}, e^z))$, and its transcendence
content is governed — *conjecturally* — by the Tannakian dimension of the
motivic Galois group of that structure.

### 3.3 The motivic Galois group of the Kummer Hodge structure

For generic $(a, b)$ with $a, b-a \notin \mathbb{Z}$, the motivic Galois group
$G_{\mathrm{mot}}(H^1_{\mathrm{irr}})$ is the Borel subgroup
$B \subset \mathrm{GL}_2$, three-dimensional. The Grothendieck Period
Conjecture (irregular version) then predicts
$$\mathrm{tr.deg}_{\mathbb{Q}}\{\text{periods of } H^1_{\mathrm{irr}}\} \;=\; \dim B \;=\; 3.$$
The three periods of the Kummer motive are
$\{u, v, S_\infty/(2\pi i)\}$ — the value, the derivative-value, and the
Stokes ratio. At $(a,b) = (1/3, 4/3)$ the third period is $1/3 \in \mathbb{Q}$,
so the GPC prediction collapses to
$\mathrm{tr.deg}_{\mathbb{Q}}\{u, v\} = 2$, matching Siegel-Shidlovskii at
this algebraic $z_0 = 1/3$.

Adding $\pi$ to the mix — as Target K does — asks: *does the irregular Hodge
structure at $\infty$ contribute any $\pi$-dependent period beyond what the
regular SS picture already supplies?* The closed form $S_\infty = 2\pi i / 3$
answers this: yes, $\pi$ appears, but only through the rational coefficient
of the Γ-ratio. The content of Target K's `NO_RELATION` verdict is that no
*additional* $\pi$-multiplicative structure (e.g. a secret relation
$\pi^2 u = q_1 u + q_2 v + q_3$ for rationals $q_i$) is consistent with the
numerics at height $10^{200}$.

## 4. Why Attack 20 Bypasses Decoupling

### 4.1 The Decoupling barrier, briefly

Every prior attack (Attacks 13, 14, 15, 19, and the core K₂-opacity argument
itself) builds Hermite-Padé / simultaneous-Padé linear forms and then invokes
Siegel's lemma to produce integer coefficients of controlled height. Paper
[04F-Final-Analysis] and [Paper-K2-Opacity-Pi] isolate the resulting
*Decoupling bottleneck*: any Siegel-lemma construction over the field
$\mathbb{Q}(\pi)$ produces denominators whose growth is governed by an
exponential factor $C^n$ tied to the transcendence measure $\mu(\pi)$, and
this $C^n$ factor is exactly what prevents the resulting linear form from
being small enough to force a rational identity. The K₂-opacity statement is
precisely that this exponential factor *cannot* be eliminated by any
Siegel-lemma-style construction.

### 4.2 Stokes data are not Siegel-lemma constructed

The crucial observation for Attack 20 is: $S_\infty$ is **not** obtained by a
Hermite-Padé or Siegel-lemma procedure. It is an *analytic invariant* of the
irregular singularity of the Kummer connection, available in closed form from
Γ-functions (equation (1.1)). There is no auxiliary polynomial construction,
no height control via Siegel's lemma, no Decoupling bottleneck. The Stokes
multiplier falls directly out of the asymptotic expansion of $M(a, b, z)$
at $z = \infty$.

In Dettweiler-Reiter terms: the Stokes cocycle is constructed
*Tannakian-motivically* from the rigid local system, not analytically from
Padé approximations. This severs the link to the K₂-opacity obstruction.
Whatever Attack 20 accomplishes (or fails to accomplish), it does so on a
*different plane* from the Decoupling bottleneck.

### 4.3 What remains

Severing the link to Decoupling is not the same as producing a transcendence
theorem. Attack 20 still needs a *motivic irreducibility / consistency*
statement — the Dettweiler-Reiter analogue of GPC for irregular Hodge
structures. That statement is *GAP_H*. It is not the K₂-opacity bottleneck;
it is a separate, open problem that the Hodge-theoretic / irregular-periods
community has been working on since Sabbah-Yu [SY15] and continuing through
Kontsevich-Soibelman [KS20].

## 5. *GAP_H* — The Remaining Theorem

### 5.1 The missing statement

**Conjecture 5.1** (*GAP_H* — Hodge consistency for Kummer Stokes data).
*Let $(a, b) \in \mathbb{Q}^2$ with $a, b-a \notin \mathbb{Z}_{\le 0}$, and let
$z_0 \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}} \cap (0, 1)$. Let*
$$u = {}_1F_1(a; b; z_0), \qquad v = \left.\partial_z \, {}_1F_1(a; b; z)\right|_{z=z_0},$$
*and let $S_\infty$ denote the Kummer Stokes multiplier at $z = \infty$. Any
algebraic relation*
$$P(u, v, \pi, S_\infty) \;=\; 0, \qquad P \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}[X_1, X_2, X_3, X_4],$$
*is forced by the closed form $S_\infty = 2\pi i \, \Gamma(b)/(\Gamma(a) \Gamma(b-a))$
together with Siegel-Shidlovskii on the pair $(u, v)$ at the algebraic point
$z_0$. In particular, at $(a, b, z_0) = (1/3, 4/3, 1/3)$, any such $P$ lies in
the ideal $(3 X_4 - 2 X_3)$ of $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}[X_1, X_2, X_3, X_4]$.*

For generic $(a, b)$ the Γ-ratio is transcendental (the Chowla-Selberg formula
provides the framework; see [Ko17, §4]), and Conjecture 5.1 reduces to the
classical GPC statement $\mathrm{tr.deg}_{\mathbb{Q}}\{u, v, \pi, S_\infty/(2\pi i)\} = 3$.
For our slice $(1/3, 4/3, 1/3)$, where the ratio is rational, the conjecture
reduces to the weaker Siegel-Shidlovskii-plus-closed-form statement, and
Target K provides the empirical support.

### 5.2 Three tractable ingredients

| Ingredient | Description | Status |
|:-----------|:------------|:-------|
| (i) Sabbah-Yu irregular Hodge | Stokes multipliers are periods of the irregular Hodge structure $H^1_{\mathrm{irr}}$ | **Proven** [SY15, Theorem 1.1] |
| (ii) Dettweiler-Reiter motivic realisation | Rigid local systems of Kummer type lift to the category of motives with Galois group $B$ | **Proven** [DR07, §3] |
| (iii) GPC for irregular Hodge structures at the Kummer slice | $\mathrm{tr.deg}_{\mathbb{Q}}(\text{periods of } H^1_{\mathrm{irr}}) = \dim G_{\mathrm{mot}}$ | **Open** (GPC-irregular in general) |

Ingredients (i) and (ii) are theorems. Ingredient (iii) is the irregular
analogue of the Grothendieck Period Conjecture, open at roughly the same
level as classical GPC but with a cleaner Tannakian story thanks to
Kontsevich-Soibelman [KS20] and the Katz-style rigidity of the Kummer local
system [Ka90, §3.2].

### 5.3 Why this is worth stating separately from GAP_M

Attack 14 (*GAP_M*, [Paper-Attack14]) targets the *regular* motivic story:
the $B \times \mathrm{SL}_2$ product of Kummer (regular, $(a,b,z_0) = (1/2, 3/2, 1/2)$
there) and Legendre. It requires classical GPC + Zilber-Pink.

Attack 20 (*GAP_H*) targets the *irregular* Hodge story: the Stokes multipliers
at $z = \infty$. It requires GPC-irregular + Dettweiler-Reiter motivic
consistency. The two Gaps are *not equivalent*:

1. GAP_M uses regular periods only (values at algebraic $z_0 \in (0,1)$ plus the
   Legendre period $\pi/4$); it sidesteps irregular-singularity data.
2. GAP_H uses *irregular* periods (Stokes data at $z = \infty$); it sidesteps
   the regular-period route entirely and gives a *different* angle on why
   $\pi$ is $K_2$-opaque with respect to $u, v$.

A reader who believes neither GAP_M nor GAP_H can still be forced into
believing $K_2$-opacity if *both* survive their respective stress tests at
matching precision — the two gaps are essentially independent sources of
evidence.

### 5.4 What precisely is missing

To upgrade Attack 20 from conjecture to theorem:

(a) A proof of GPC for the irregular Hodge structure
    $H^1_{\mathrm{irr}}(\mathbb{A}^1, \nabla_{a,b} \otimes e^z)$ at the
    Kummer parameters $(1/3, 4/3)$. This is strictly weaker than full GPC for
    Shimura varieties but strictly stronger than anything currently available
    in the Sabbah-Yu / Kontsevich-Soibelman framework.

(b) Failing (a): a Tannakian dimension-count for the motivic Galois group of
    the irregular Hodge structure that takes into account the specific rational
    Γ-ratio at $(1/3, 4/3)$ — essentially, checking that the Stokes multiplier
    reducing to $2\pi i / 3$ does not *coincidentally* force an extra relation
    on $(u, v)$.

Neither (a) nor (b) is on the near horizon; GPC-irregular is roughly as open
as classical GPC, perhaps slightly less so in the rank-two Kummer case
[KS20, §4.3]. The value of this note is *that it cleanly isolates the
irregular-Hodge gap*, independently of the regular motivic route of
Attack 14.

## 6. Summary

| Statement | Proven? | Reference |
|:----------|:--------|:----------|
| $S_\infty = 2\pi i \, \Gamma(b)/(\Gamma(a)\Gamma(b-a))$ (closed form) | Proven | [AS72 §13.5]; [vdPS03 Thm 8.10] |
| Stokes data are periods of the irregular Hodge structure | Proven | [SY15, Thm 1.1] |
| Kummer rigid local system has a motivic realisation with Galois group $B$ | Proven | [DR07, §3]; [Ka90, §3.2] |
| $\mathrm{tr.deg}_{\mathbb{Q}}\{u, v\} = 2$ at algebraic $z_0 \in (0,1)$ | Proven | [Si49], [Sh56], [Be06] |
| No $\mathbb{Q}$-relation of the closed-form-quotiented 8-tuple (Stage C), height $\le 10^{200}$ | Proven | Target K (Session 15) |
| GPC for the irregular Hodge structure at $(1/3, 4/3)$ | **Open** | Conj. 5.1, *GAP_H* (iii) |
| Any relation $P(u, v, \pi, S_\infty) = 0$ reduces to the closed-form ideal | **Open** | Conjecture 5.1 |

Attack 20 isolates the **irregular** Hodge-monodromy route to $K_2$-opacity.
Its empirical anchor (Target K, `NO_RELATION` verdict on the 8-monomial
closed-form-quotient tuple) matches the quality of Target I2 for Attack 14 and
Target H for Attack 12. The structural content — that Stokes data are
*not* constructed by Siegel's lemma and therefore bypass Decoupling — is what
makes this attack *topologically distinct* from the Hermite-Padé family of
attacks. The remaining theorem (*GAP_H*) is GPC for an irregular Hodge
structure, which is open but in principle addressable by the
Sabbah-Yu / Kontsevich-Soibelman / Dettweiler-Reiter circle.

Across the four companion papers, the $K_2$-opacity route is now framed via
four independent Galois-theoretic attacks, each with its own cleanly-isolated
gap:

- **Attack 13 — *GAP_L*** (Lucas / $p$-adic): specialisation from $\mathbb{Q}(z)$
  to $\mathbb{Q}$ for mixed $E \oplus G$ pairs.
- **Attack 12 — *GAP_C*** (Carlitz-Frobenius / Mahler): mixed Mahler $\times$
  Fuchsian specialisation extending Nishioka 1996.
- **Attack 14 — *GAP_M*** (regular motivic / Zilber-Pink): GPC + Zilber-Pink for
  the $B \times \mathrm{SL}_2$ product motive.
- **Attack 20 — *GAP_H*** (irregular Hodge / Stokes): GPC-irregular for the
  Kummer Stokes structure at $z = \infty$.

Four gaps, four *structurally independent* Galois-theoretic routes, four
matching `NO_RELATION` empirical anchors. Attack 20 is not alive because we
have a proof; it is alive because the route from data to theorem is reduced
to a single conjecture (GPC-irregular) in a framework where partial results
exist [SY15, KS20, DR07].
