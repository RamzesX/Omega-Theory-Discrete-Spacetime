# OmegaTheory V2 — Lean 4 Formalization

Machine-verified formalization of Omega-Theory / Chaos Shield discrete quantum gravity.

**Two central results:**

1. Einstein's field equations `G_μν = (8πG/c⁴)T_μν + O(l_P)` emerge from a discrete
   Planck-scale lattice with computational truncation errors and self-healing
   dynamics — at healing-flow equilibrium, and modulo the HPW axiom (now provably
   replaceable via the `HpwEliminableRegime` typeclass on 3 regimes).

2. **Non-relativistic quantum mechanics emerges as theorems** from the same substrate.
   The capstone `grand_qm_emergence` in `Emergence/QuantumMechanicsCapstone.lean`
   bundles seven von-Neumann postulates + Bell/CHSH entanglement into a single
   machine-checked statement: Schrödinger evolution, Born-rule conservation,
   non-relativistic-limit, two-slit interference, Heisenberg uncertainty,
   measurement/collapse, and `chshTsirelsonBell = 2√2` exceeding the classical
   Bell bound of 2 — all derived from a `DynamicalSnapshotSequence` under
   `HasZeroFunctional`. First machine-checked complete derivation of
   non-relativistic QM from a discrete gravitational substrate.


```
Conservation → HealingFlow → Emergence → Predictions →
```

`einstein_with_matter_emergence` lives in `OmegaTheory/Emergence/EinsteinEmergence.lean`
and reduces in one line to `ricci_from_equilibrium_balance`, which in turn uses
the healing-flow equilibrium condition plus the HPW axiom.
