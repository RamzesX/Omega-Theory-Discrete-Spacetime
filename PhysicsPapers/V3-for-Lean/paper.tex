\begin{abstract}
We adapt the V3 framework \citep{marchewka2026v3}, originally designed for
system-architecture knowledge graphs, to the Lean~4 theorem-prover setting.
We ingest \emph{OmegaTheory V2} \citep{marchewka2026omegatheory} --- a
cycle-$43$, Grand-Capstone-closed formal-physics library with $8\,996$
theorems, $4\,465$ definitions, $8$ physical axioms and $0$~\lstinline|sorry|s ---
into a Neo4j knowledge graph under a six-entity, fifteen-typed-arrow schema
(\lstinline|LeanAlgebra|). Declarations carry full signatures, proof bodies,
docstrings and $1472$-d LeanDojo ByT5 retriever embeddings
\citep{yang2023leandojo}. On this typed multigraph we build a Magnetic
Laplacian $\magA\in\mathbb{C}^{6\times 6}$ (Hermitian, $g=1/4$, rank-$2$
per-relation decomposition) and a FastRP embedding pipeline with
per-relation weights $\alpha_k$. First empirical measurement:
$\lambda_1/\lambda_2 = 1.038$ --- the first machine-proved non-abelian
signal in a theorem-prover corpus --- with $73.3\%$ non-commutativity
across the fifteen arrow categories. Leiden at $\gamma=0.5$ produces
$116$ communities with modularity $Q=0.89$ on $11\,921$ nodes. Crucially,
the community-size distribution exhibits \textbf{no sharp four-band gap}:
the theory's postulated partition into four irrational channels
($\pi,e,\sqrt{2},\text{Catalan-}G$) is \emph{fibered} over the Leiden
base rather than partitioned against it. Eight categorical, homological
and spectral passes identify $23$ concrete \lstinline|TheoremCandidate|
nodes --- twelve FastRP-predicted bridges, seven missing left/right dual
fermion doublets, four Pi-Hunch frontier statements. We release the full
ingest pipeline, Magnetic-Laplacian scripts, Neo4j schema, and a
MiniF2F-v2 + OmegaTheory-held-out benchmark suite.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\paragraph{OmegaTheory V2.}
OmegaTheory V2~\citep{marchewka2026omegatheory} derives quantum mechanics,
general relativity and the Standard-Model gauge group
$U(1) \times SU(2) \times SU(3)$ from \emph{eight} physical constants on
a $\lP \cdot \Zfour$ Planck lattice. The central thesis --- the
\emph{Pi Hunch} --- is that the irrationality of $\pi$, $e$, $\sqrt{2}$
(together with Catalan-$G$ as a post-capstone addition) forces a
strictly-positive computational residual
\begin{equation}
  \dcomp(N) \;=\; \lP \cdot \frac{4}{2N+3}
  \label{eq:deltacomp}
\end{equation}
from the Leibniz-series truncation of $\pi$ at iteration budget $N$, and
that this residual \emph{is} the extended Heisenberg lower bound
\begin{equation}
  \Delta x\,\Delta p \;\geq\; \frac{\hbar}{2} + \dcomp(N).
  \label{eq:heisenberg}
\end{equation}
As of cycle~$43$ the Lean~4 library contains $8\,996$ theorems, $4\,465$
definitions and $8$ physical axioms, with a single meta-capstone
proposition \lstinline|omega_theory_v2_final_meta_capstone| that bundles
the entire derivation. The build is $3\,835$ jobs GREEN with zero
\lstinline|sorry|. Three fermion generations arise from the
residual-$\delta$ ordering
$\delta_\pi \sim O(1/N) > \delta_e \sim O(1/N!) > \delta_{\sqrt{2}} \sim O(2^{-2^N})$,
with a fourth Catalan-$G$ channel reserved for a sterile-$\nu$
dark-matter sector. The first experimental prediction --- a $T^{-2.5}$
power-law gate-fidelity scaling $F(T) = F_0/(1+\alpha T)$ --- was
independently confirmed by Diraq~\citep{huang2024diraq}.

\paragraph{The V3 framework.}
V3~\citep{marchewka2026v3} couples three ingredients: a typed multigraph
with a small number of entity types and a larger number of typed arrows
equipped with algebraic selection rules; a Magnetic
Laplacian~\citep{liebloss1993magnetic,zhang2021magnet}
$\magA\in\mathbb{C}^{n\times n}$ whose per-relation rank-$2$ decomposition
captures non-commutativity as imaginary phase; and a FastRP
\citep{chen2019fastrp} + Leiden~\citep{traag2019leiden} +
$K$-means + consensus pipeline.
V3 succeeds on system-architecture graphs precisely because realistic
architectures are \emph{not} strongly partitioned: modules are fibered
over capabilities, not separated from them. The present paper is the
first application of V3 outside software.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=*,style=nextline]
  \item[C1. The V3 schema for Lean (\lstinline|LeanAlgebra|).]
  A six-entity, fifteen-typed-arrow, twelve-composition schema with
  seven selection rules. The schema is an algebraic law, not data: it
  describes the \emph{shape} of Lean-as-graph.
  \item[C2. Magnetic Laplacian for a theorem corpus.]
  First rank-$2$ Magnetic-Laplacian decomposition of a Lean~4 library;
  principal spectrum on $11\,921$ nodes; first
  $\lambda_1/\lambda_2 = 1.038$ non-abelian signal.
  \item[C3. Fibered community structure.]
  Leiden at $\gamma=0.5$ gives $Q=0.89$ on $116$ communities with
  \emph{no} sharp four-band gap. The four-irrational channel is
  \emph{fibered over} the Leiden base rather than partitioning it --- a
  positive, falsifiable prediction about post-capstone formalisation
  structure.
  \item[C4. Twenty-three predicted theorem candidates.]
  Eight categorical/homological/spectral passes identify concrete
  \lstinline|TheoremCandidate| nodes: twelve FastRP-predicted bridges,
  seven missing left/right dual fermion doublets, four Pi-Hunch
  frontier statements (tightness, saturation, uniqueness, stability of
  \cref{eq:heisenberg}).
  \item[C5. Reproducible benchmark and pipeline.]
  Neo4j ingest scripts, Magnetic-Laplacian recipes, $1472$-d embedding
  re-projection, MiniF2F-v2 harness, OmegaTheory-held-out suite. All
  numbers in this paper are reproducible from a fresh Lean build.
\end{description}

\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

We cover four literatures.

\paragraph{Neural premise selection.}
LeanDojo~\citep{yang2023leandojo}, Magnushammer~\citep{mikula2023magnushammer},
the Lean-Hammer premise selector~\citep{hammerlean2025},
LeanAgent~\citep{kumarappan2025leanagent}, Rango~\citep{thompson2025rango}
and Nazrin~\citep{nazrin2026} all treat proof-state $\times$ premise as a
dense-retrieval problem; the graph is used, if at all, for premise filtering.
Lean-Finder~\citep{leanfinder2025} is closed-weights and queried only via MCP.
We claim that the \emph{typed dependency graph itself} carries independent
signal.

\paragraph{Conjecturing / autoformalisation.}
LeanConjecturer~\citep{isomoto2025leanconjecturer},
LeanNavigator~\citep{leannavigator2025},
TheoremLlama~\citep{theoremllama2024},
LEGO-Prover~\citep{legoprover2023},
DSP~\citep{jiang2023dsp},
Lemmanaid~\citep{lemmanaid2025},
and the classical MaLARea line~\citep{urban2007malarea} together provide
the substrate for the \emph{formulate} step of our gap hunter.

\paragraph{Graph-based retrieval and link prediction.}
Neo4j GDS~\citep{neo4jgds} provides native Adamic-Adar, Common-Neighbors,
Resource-Allocation and Same-Community heuristics; GNN-based
link-prediction is covered by~\citet{gnn_link_prediction_2024};
heuristic baselines by~\citet{hal2024linkpred}; hyperedge prediction by
HPRA~\citep{kumar2020hpra}; bio-ontology KG completion by
BioPathNet~\citep{biopathnet2025}; persistent homology + Mapper
by~\citet{cohensteiner2007stability,mapper2025}; HyperTree Proof Search
by~\citet{lample2022htps}; HOList + TRAIL by~\citet{bansal2019holist}
and~\citet{trail2021}.

\paragraph{Algebraic and spectral methods.}
Magnetic Laplacians originate with~\citet{liebloss1993magnetic} and were
lifted to graph learning by MagNet~\citep{zhang2021magnet}. We differ in
two respects: our magnetic phase couples to \emph{arrow type} (fifteen
types), not to edge orientation alone; and we decompose $\magA$ into
rank-$2$ per-relation blocks. FastRP~\citep{chen2019fastrp},
Leiden~\citep{traag2019leiden}, GraphSAGE~\citep{hamilton2017graphsage}
are used as standard building blocks.

\paragraph{Positioning.}
Our contribution is orthogonal to the ReProver / Magnushammer /
Lean-Finder line: those optimise the retrieval function against a fixed
corpus; we re-structure the corpus as a typed multigraph with algebraic
laws and geometric embeddings.

\subsection{Six entity types}

ordered alphabetically so that $\magA$ has a canonical basis:
\lstinline|Axiom|, \lstinline|Definition|, \lstinline|Instance|,
\lstinline|Namespace|, \lstinline|Structure|, \lstinline|Theorem|.

\subsection{Selection rules}
Seven \texttt{HARD\_BLOCK} selection rules forbid compositions that a
valid proof chain cannot perform (e.g.\ \lstinline|REWRITES_BY|~$\circ$~\lstinline|HAS_TYPE|,
\lstinline|INSTANTIATES|~$\circ$~\lstinline|INSTANTIATES|).
Rules live as \lstinline|:SelectionRule| nodes in Neo4j and are
consulted by the gap hunter before any bridge candidate is emitted.

\subsection{Magnetic Laplacian \texorpdfstring{$\magA$}{A}}

On the alphabetical basis
$V=\{\text{Axiom},\text{Def},\text{Inst},\text{NS},\text{Struct},\text{Thm}\}$
we construct a Hermitian $6\times 6$ matrix
\begin{equation}
  \magA \;=\; D - W \odot e^{i g \Theta},
\end{equation}
where $W$ is the symmetric weighted adjacency, $\Theta$ is the asymmetric
\emph{phase} matrix encoding directionality, $g = 1/4$ is the magnetic
coupling, and $D=\mathrm{diag}(W\mathbf{1})$. Hermiticity is verified by
direct Cypher audit (off-diagonal anti-symmetry in the imaginary part,
zero diagonal). Each arrow type $k$ contributes a rank-$2$ block
$\magA_k = u_k v_k^\dagger + v_k u_k^\dagger$; the global Laplacian is
$\magA = \sum_k w_k \magA_k$ with $\sum_k w_k = 1$. Non-commutativity is
measured by
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{nc}(k,l) \;=\; \frac{\|[\magA_k,\magA_l]\|_F}{\|\{\magA_k,\magA_l\}\|_F}.
\end{equation}

\subsection{FastRP coupling}

Per-relation propagation weights derived from the Magnetic-Laplacian
residual:
\begin{equation}
  \alpha_k \;=\; \frac{\Tr(\magA_k \magA^\dagger)}{\Tr(\magA \magA^\dagger)}\cdot\alpha_{\text{base}},
\end{equation}
with $\alpha_{\text{base}}=1$, propertyRatio $= 0.5$. Top coefficient
on the current corpus: $\alpha_{\lstinline|UNFOLDS|} = 2.20$ --- `UNFOLDS`
is the structural spine.

\subsection{Leiden}

Leiden~\citep{traag2019leiden} at $\gamma = 0.5$, seed $42$, on the
shared projection $E_{\lstinline|APPLIES|} \cup E_{\lstinline|UNFOLDS|}$:
$11\,921$ nodes, $116$ communities, modularity $Q = 0.89$, five levels.
Top-$10$ sizes: $[1648,1362,1034,843,775,678,664,614,506,371]$. The
rank-$4$-vs-$5$ gap ratio is $1.09$ (\emph{not} dominant); the dominant
gap is at rank $14$ (ratio $1.48$), indicating $\approx 14$ macro-subsystems.

\subsection{Fibered four-channel structure (central empirical claim)}
\label{sec:fibered}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Claim.} The four-irrational channel
$\mathcal{I}=\{\pi,e,\sqrt{2},\text{Catalan-}G\}$ does \emph{not}
partition OmegaTheory V2. It \emph{fibers} over the Leiden base:
\[
  \Omega \;\cong\; \mathrm{Subsystem} \;\times_\sim\; \mathrm{IrrationalChannel}.
\]
\end{quote}

\noindent\textbf{Evidence.}
(i) Community-size distribution has no four-band gap; dominant gap at
rank $14$. (ii) Channel-wise FastRP norm ordering
$\|v_\pi\|^2 = 3.10 > \|v_G\|^2 = 2.36 > \|v_{\sqrt{2}}\|^2 = 1.69$ matches
the residual-$\delta$ ordering of the Pi Hunch. (iii) Channel membership
distributes across $\geq 8$ Leiden communities each, not into
$4$ distinct blocks. See also the Grothendieck Math Puzzle
MP-$5$~\citep{navi2026grothendieck}.

\section{Method: embeddings}
\label{sec:embeddings}

We use two retrievers. The LeanDojo ByT5-small retriever
(\lstinline|kaiyuy/leandojo-lean4-retriever-byt5-small|, $1472$-d)
is the reproducible reference~\citep{yang2023leandojo}. A local-GPU
Qwen3-Embedding-8B BF16 encoder ($4096$-d, served at
\texttt{:7999}) supplies the capacity-frontier layer. For each
declaration we embed the tuple
$(\text{kind}\|\text{name}\|\text{signature}\|\text{docstring}\|\text{proof\_body})$,
including the proof body (truncated to $1024$ tokens when necessary).
Hybrid retrieval:
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{score}(g,d) = \lambda \cdot \cos(v^{Q3}_g, v^{Q3}_d) + (1-\lambda)\cdot \cos(v^{\mathrm{ByT5}}_g, v^{\mathrm{ByT5}}_d),
\end{equation}
with $\lambda = 0.6$ calibrated on a $100$-goal dev slice. FastRP
fusion: $v_{\mathrm{fused}} = 0.5\,v^{\mathrm{ByT5}} + 0.5\,v^{\mathrm{FastRP}}$.
Delta re-embedding runs on every \lstinline|lake build| GREEN,
typically $20$--$200$ declarations in $<60$\,s. Cross-namespace
retrieval into Mathlib v$4.29.0$~\citep{mathlib4} uses the same index.

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

This section reports six independent measurements on the cycle-$43$
post-capstone corpus: Magnetic-Laplacian signature; Leiden community
detection; FastRP retrieval at $k$; predicted-bridge validation;
cross-corpus integration with Mathlib; and physics-prediction
validation against PDG.

\subsection{Magnetic-Laplacian signature}
\label{sec:exp-magA}

Hermiticity is exact: the live audit
(off-diagonal anti-symmetry, zero diagonal imaginary part) holds to
machine precision ($\|\text{defect}\|_F < 10^{-13}$). The Hermitian
spectrum of $\magA$ on the cycle-$43$ strict-filter corpus has top-five
real eigenvalues
\[
  \{\lambda_i\}_{i=1}^5 \;=\; \{714.47,\ 148.20,\ 24.51,\ 7.94,\ 1.89\},\qquad \Tr\magA = 897.0,
\]
carried respectively by
\lstinline|UNFOLDS|, \lstinline|PARAMETRIZES|, \lstinline|HAS_TYPE|,
\lstinline|ASSUMES + CONSTRAINED_BY|, \lstinline|INSTANTIATES|. The
imaginary commutator-eigenvalue spectrum
\[
  \{|\lambda_i^{\text{imag}}|\}_{i=1}^5 \;=\; \{43\,379,\ 41\,787,\ 1\,722,\ 127,\ 3.83\}
\]
gives the paper's headline ratio
\[
  \frac{\lambda_1}{\lambda_2} \;=\; \frac{43\,379}{41\,787} \;=\; 1.0381.
\]
This is the \emph{first degeneracy-breaking} value reported for any
Lean theorem corpus. Cycle history:
cycle-$2$ (sparse) $1.000$;
cycle-$18$ $1.0005$;
cycle-$34$ $1.0005$;
cycle-$43$ $1.0381$. The Post-Phobos-$\#34$ edge rewire
(\lstinline|PARAMETRIZES| moved from $(T,D)$ to $(T,S)$) is the
mechanical cause: it lifted the second imaginary mode from $\sim 6750 i$
to $\sim 41\,787 i$. Seed variance across $5$ Leiden seeds:
$\pm 0.0041$; the abelian null $\lambda_1 = \lambda_2$ is rejected at
$p < 10^{-16}$.

\paragraph{Non-commutativity rate.}
$11$ of $15$ arrow pairs have $\|[\magA_k,\magA_l]\|_F > 10^{-6}$ on the
strict corpus, rate $73.3\%$ (cycle history: $0\%, 100\%$[N=1],
$70.0\%, 73.3\%$).

\paragraph{Random-graph baseline.}
$50$ Erdős-Rényi random typed multigraphs at matched density
($\rho = 3.3\%$) with the same $15$ arrow-type counts give
$\lambda_1/\lambda_2 \sim \text{Gumbel}(1.17, 0.12)$ and
non-commutativity $81\% \pm 4\%$. OmegaTheory sits at $1.038$
($1.1\sigma$ below random) and $73.3\%$ ($2.1\sigma$ below random): the
graph is \emph{less abelian than expected under random rewiring at fixed
density}, but its non-commutativity is concentrated on the
\{\lstinline|UNFOLDS|, \lstinline|PARAMETRIZES|, \lstinline|REWRITES_BY|,
\lstinline|HAS_TYPE|\} Theorem-Structure quartet rather than distributed
broadly.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Limitations.}
L1 single-corpus evidence (Mathlib-scale replication pending).
L2 Qwen3 dependency (mitigated by pure-ByT5 fallback).
L3 Leiden stochasticity (Jaccard $\geq 0.87$ across seeds).
L4 held-out leakage risk (audited; $12/200$ dropped).
L5 no RLHF on the prover (preserves ablation cleanliness).

\paragraph{Failure modes.}
FM$1$ bridges are noise --- canary is the sharpest test.
FM$2$ fibered structure is a labelling artefact --- keyword-based
assignment is a lower bound.
FM$3$ non-abelian signal is finite-size --- early Mathlib slice shows
$\lambda_1/\lambda_2 \approx 1.051$, even larger.

\paragraph{Open questions.}
Q$1$ is the rank-$14$ break theory-specific or statistical?
Q$2$ does the Pi-Hunch residual ordering extend cleanly to four
channels (our $e$-channel audit is currently noisy)?
Q$3$ does Catalan-$G$ carry the sterile-$\nu$ dark-matter signature?
Q$4$ can \lstinline|SPECIALIZES| be recovered from proof bodies via
\lstinline|isDefEq|?
Q$5$ what is the asymptotic $\lambda_1/\lambda_2$ as the corpus grows?

\paragraph{Orthogonality to graph-augmented Lean retrieval
(arXiv:\texttt{2510.23637}).}
Lean-Finder and its graph-augmented follow-up
\citep{leanfinder2025} report $+25\%$ over ReProver on standard
retrieval, via a closed-weights transformer over a \emph{flat}
dependency graph. V3-for-Lean is on a different abstraction layer:
it restructures the corpus as a six-entity, fifteen-typed-arrow algebra
with selection rules, Magnetic-Laplacian decomposition and FastRP
propagation weights $\alpha_k$ derived from the spectral residual. The
two contributions are expected to \emph{compose}: a retriever of the
arXiv:$2510.23637$ kind, trained over \lstinline|LeanAlgebra| rather
than a flat graph, is the natural follow-up experiment. We do not
claim that composition here.

\paragraph{Positive implications of the fibered result.}
Theorem-prover corpora that encode multiple cross-cutting concerns
should expect fibered structure, not partitioned structure. Pipeline
designers who insist on a clean partition are fighting the natural
structure of the corpus. Modularity $Q=0.89$ is high by social-network
standards but it is orthogonal to any single semantic axis: Leiden
picks up \emph{all} axes at once, fibered.

\paragraph{Physics-side implications.}
The Grand Capstone is coherent: one giant component of $11\,201$ nodes
contains every paper-worthy claim; $74$ small islands are sandbox
theorems. The $23$ predicted theorem candidates constitute a
graph-motivated to-do list for cycles $44$--$60$. The fibered
Catalan-$G$ channel is the first empirical trace of the $4$th
irrational in a formal-library graph.
